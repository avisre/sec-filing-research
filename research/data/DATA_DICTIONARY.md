# Data dictionary

## row-observations.csv.gz

UTF-8 CSV compressed with gzip. One row per security, statement, reporting basis and metric. The export contains 852,416 rows; 76 presentation metrics per security per basis.

- `symbol`: security ticker from the frozen product index; not an issuer key.
- `cik`: zero-padded SEC issuer identifier as a string. Empty means unresolved, not zero.
- `name`: public security/issuer display name.
- `tier`: original product rule, 1 for non-ADR securities marked recent annual reporters with quarterly cadence; 2 otherwise. Not independently recertified.
- `archetype`: product industry classification used by exclusion rules.
- `statement`: income, balance or cash.
- `basis`: annual or quarterly.
- `metric`: presentation row label; not a guarantee of accounting equivalence to a source tag.
- `role`: row, dropdown or in-dropdown. Included to preserve the original aggregation grain.
- `status`: filled (all available records populated), gappy (some populated), hidden (none populated/no statement records).
- `reason`: single original diagnostic when not filled; empty for filled rows. A heuristic label, not an adjudicated cause.
- `candidate_tag`: tag that triggered the original source-match heuristic, when available. No implied semantic equivalence.
- `excluded_by_original_rule`: true for not_applicable, per_share_withheld or formula_blank_by_design; false otherwise.

The audit's reason matcher accepted several filing forms, matched period ends within four days, required 340–390 days for annual duration facts, and used instantaneous facts for balance-sheet rows. Quarterly duration heuristics differed for cash flow. These rules explain possible comparability limits; they are not claims that every resulting match is appropriate. Period counts and exact dates are absent from the original detailed labels, so full-panel five-year completeness cannot be reconstructed from this file.

## Original summaries

`original-by-stock.csv` and `original-by-row.csv` are frozen aggregate exports used as independent reconciliation targets. Annual filled and gappy counts exclude the original structural reasons in the security summary. Raw row aggregates include them, so the two denominators must not be mixed. `missing_annual_rows` encodes row-level diagnostic labels, not financial values. The quarterly security summary contains filled/total counts only; detailed observations supply the three-way split.

## Evidence and sample

`selection-protocol.json` states the fixed sampling rule. `results/selected-cases.csv` is regenerated by analyze.py. `case-review.csv` / `.json` retain all 30 judgments. `fact-checks.csv` records 27 original-document numeric facts with dates, units, dimensions and hashes. Numeric receipt values are as encoded in the source, before product presentation transformations such as cash-outflow sign changes. The other three cases use narrative or reporting-basis evidence.

`sample-period-values.json` preserves null distinctly from numeric zero and lists the available annual dates for the sample. These are product-projected values, not independently validated values for every period. `filing-references.csv` records direct SEC document URLs and hashes; quarterly comparative fact dates are in fact-checks.csv and differ from the quarterly filing's report period. `aac-filing-history.csv` is a field-limited SEC submissions-history projection; its source hash and retrieval scope are in aac-history-provenance.json.

## Results

`coverage.csv` contains numerator counts and denominators by tier, basis and statement. Rates are percentages rounded to four decimal places. Paper tables round to one decimal place. `summary.json` contains exact census counts, diagnostic totals, exclusions, reconciliation results and issuer-weighting sensitivity. No confidence intervals or sample error rate are estimated because the cases are purposive, not random.
